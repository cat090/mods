# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep the list up to date!

## Adding a mod

1. Make sure it isn't listed already.
2. Use the rules below to pick allowed or banned.
3. Add it next to similar mods, in alphabetical order.
4. Link Modrinth if it's there, otherwise CurseForge or the official GitHub.
5. Open a pull request and say why it belongs there.

Not sure where a mod fits? Open an issue and we'll figure it out together.

## Rules

### Allowed

- Cosmetics that don't give an unfair edge, like capes and crosshairs.
- Performance mods that leave gameplay alone.
- HUDs that show info about you, like your armor, potions and saturation.
- Anything that only restyles what you can already see, like hit color or hitboxes.

### Banned

- Changing movement or reach.
- Automating things, like auto clickers, macros and auto totem.
- Removing vanilla delays, like the shield or hotbar delay.
- Making your client act unlike vanilla, like walking in inventories or changed block placement.
- Making opponents easier to spot, like ESP, radar and freelook.
- Showing info vanilla doesn't, like opponent health, reach indicators or trajectories.
- Putting several actions on one key. Mice that need it for button binding are fine.
- Hurting your connection or ping.

A mod with even one banned feature is banned, whatever it's called.
